YOUR
DUTIES.

No fluff. Here is exactly what you must do to earn your incentives and climb the leaderboard.

01
Share Opportunity in Your Campus Network
Distribute ORIGENNT's program links and forms in WhatsApp groups, college communities, hostels, and classrooms consistently and authentically.

Collect & Submit Verified Student Leads
Gather interested students' details with their consent. Submit valid, genuine leads only — no spam, no fake entries. Authenticity is your reputation.
