Common Questions

Everything you
need to know before
you start.

Clear answers to the questions every client asks. If yours isn't here, our team responds to enquiries within 4 hours.

Do you work with international clients?+
Yes. We actively work with clients in the UK, UAE, USA, Singapore, and Australia. All projects are managed remotely with weekly video calls, and we can bill in GBP, AED, or USD. We've delivered projects across 8+ countries without a single on-site meeting.
What does "60-day free support" actually mean?+
It means 60 calendar days of complimentary support after your site goes live — covering bug fixes, content updates, plugin issues, and performance monitoring. No hidden charges, no per-request billing. After 60 days, you can continue with one of our maintenance plans.
Do I own the code after delivery?+
100%. On final payment, we transfer all source code, assets, and documentation to you. You are never locked into using ORIGENNT for hosting, updates, or future development. The website is yours, outright.
What's the payment structure?+
We require 50% advance to begin work. The remaining 50% is due upon project completion before the final handover and DNS migration. For projects over ₹5L, we can split into three milestones. All terms are in the written project agreement.
How long does a typical website take?+
Landing pages: 1–2 weeks. 5-page WordPress sites: 2–3 weeks. 10-page corporate sites: 4–5 weeks. Next.js performance sites: 3–5 weeks. E-commerce: 4–8 weeks. Custom full-stack platforms: 12–20+ weeks. All timelines are guaranteed in writing.
Is GST charged on top of the listed prices?+
Yes. All prices shown are exclusive of GST (18%). For Indian clients, GST is added to the invoice. International clients (UK, UAE, USA, etc.) are not subject to Indian GST and are billed the listed amount in their preferred currency.
What platforms do you build on?+
WordPress (with Elementor or Gutenberg), WooCommerce, Shopify, Next.js 14, React, Node.js, and custom full-stack solutions. We recommend the right platform for your specific needs — not the one we prefer to build on.
Do you offer refunds if the project is late?+
Yes. If ORIGENNT misses an agreed milestone date without a valid, documented reason, we offer a pro-rated refund on that phase. This is written into your project agreement. We've maintained a 94% on-time delivery rate.
Can I see work examples before committing?+
Absolutely. Book a free 30-minute discovery call and we'll walk you through relevant case studies, live examples, and the specific design direction planned for your project — before any payment is required.
Do you handle hosting and domain setup?+
Yes — we can recommend, set up, and configure hosting, including managed WordPress hosts, Vercel for Next.js projects, or cloud servers (AWS/DigitalOcean). Domain transfer and DNS migration are handled during the launch phase.
