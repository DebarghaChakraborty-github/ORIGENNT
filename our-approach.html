Our Consulting Philosophy

We diagnose before
we prescribe.

Too many HR consulting engagements begin with a pre-sold solution. The client buys a product; the consultant delivers it; and the root problem remains. ORIGENNT was built to break that pattern.

We begin every engagement — regardless of size or sector — with a diagnostic. We listen before we advise. We understand the specific context of your organisation, your people, your market, and your regulatory environment before we recommend anything.

Our Memorandum of Association explicitly authorises us to provide HR consulting, payroll management, talent acquisition, performance management, and strategic advisory. We operate with full legal authority and professional rigour across all our practice areas.

01
Diagnostics over prescriptions
Every engagement starts with structured listening — stakeholder interviews, data review, compliance mapping. No solution before problem definition.
02
Capability transfer, not dependency creation
We design every engagement to make ourselves less necessary over time. Your team should own the outcomes long after we leave.
03
Data before opinion
Our recommendations are anchored in data — HR analytics, compliance benchmarks, market intelligence — not gut feel.
04
Regulatory precision
India's labour and tax landscape is complex and rapidly changing. We stay ahead of every change so our clients never have to.
05
Human outcomes first
Behind every HR metric is a person. Our work is designed to improve lives — of employees and the organisations that rely on them.

Common Questions

About how we work — answered.

Is ORIGENNT authorised to act on behalf of clients?
Yes. ORIGENNT PRIVATE LIMITED is registered under the Companies Act 2013, incorporated in Odisha on 11 November 2025. Our Memorandum of Association explicitly authorises us to provide payroll processing, compliance management, talent acquisition, strategic HR consulting, learning and development, and related HR operations on behalf of clients. All our practice areas operate within MOA objects.
Do you work with companies outside Odisha?
Yes. While ORIGENNT is headquartered in Bhubaneswar, we operate across 12+ states. Our payroll and compliance practice manages multi-state employers with employees in Odisha, West Bengal, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana, and others. Our consulting and analytics teams work remotely and on-site as required. State location is not a constraint.
What size of company does ORIGENNT work with?
Our client base ranges from startups with 8 employees to enterprises with 500+. Our payroll plans start from ₹3,000/month for up to 10 employees. Our consulting engagements scale from single-project mandates (audits, assessments) to multi-year retainer relationships. Size is not a barrier to working with ORIGENNT.
How long is a typical engagement?
It varies significantly by practice area. Payroll is a monthly recurring service — clients typically stay 12–36 months. Compliance audits are point-in-time (1–3 weeks). Consulting and transformation engagements typically run 3–9 months. Our average engagement duration across all practice areas is 14 months, and our client retention rate is 87%.
How do you charge — fixed, hourly, or retainer?
Our payroll and compliance practice uses fixed monthly pricing (per employee or per plan). Our consulting and analytics engagements are typically fixed-scope with milestone-based payments. We do not charge hourly for most work — we prefer outcome-based structures that align our incentives with your results. Custom pricing is available for large enterprises.
What happens when regulatory requirements change?
ORIGENNT maintains a regulatory watch function across all practice areas. When laws change — as they did with the November 2025 Labour Codes and the new Income Tax Act 2025 — we proactively update all client engagements, adjust payroll configurations, and brief clients on implications before deadlines arrive. You will never be caught off-guard by a regulatory change when ORIGENNT is engaged.
Do you sign NDAs and confidentiality agreements?
Yes — always. Every ORIGENNT engagement begins with a signed NDA and data confidentiality agreement. Employee payroll data, business strategy documents, and financial information shared with us is processed under strict confidentiality and never shared with third parties. We follow India's DPDP Act 2023 norms and maintain encrypted records as required by the November 2025 Labour Codes.
How do we get started?
The simplest way is a 45-minute discovery call — no commitment, no pitch, no proposal until we both agree there's a fit. You can book directly via the button above, email origennt@gmail.com, or WhatsApp. We typically confirm a call time within 4 business hours and will send a brief agenda so neither of us wastes the other's time.
