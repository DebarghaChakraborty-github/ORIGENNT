FAQ

Common questions,
straight answers.

How long does a typical project take?
Landing pages take 3–5 days. Corporate websites take 2–4 weeks. Growth and Plus packages take 4–14 weeks. Enterprise timelines are scoped per project. We always define the timeline upfront in the brief — no surprises.
Do I own my website and code after delivery?
100%. You own all code, assets, and infrastructure from day one. There is no proprietary platform lock-in. We transfer everything on final payment — documented and legally clear.
Can you work with international clients?
Yes. We serve clients in the UK, USA, UAE, Singapore, Australia, and across India. We invoice in INR, GBP, USD, and AED. Remote collaboration has never cost a client a pixel of quality.
What is your payment structure?
Super Saver: 100% upfront. Starter: 50/50. Growth and above: 40% kickoff, 30% design approval, 30% on delivery. We accept bank transfer, Razorpay, and international wire.
What is the 60-day support period?
After launch, ORIGENNT remains engaged for 60 days. We fix bugs, handle hosting issues, make minor content edits, and provide guidance on analytics and performance. After 60 days, you can move to a maintenance plan.
Do you use templates or build custom?
Zero templates. Every project is custom-designed from scratch based on your brief. We don't recycle design work between clients — brand differentiation is fundamental to what we do.
Can I add automation to an existing website?
Yes. Our standalone automation systems (WhatsApp CRM, KPI reporting, invoice tracking, social auto-posting) can integrate with most existing websites and business tools via API. We audit your current stack before quoting.
How do I get started?
Hit "Start a Project" anywhere on this page. You'll fill a brief form and we respond within 4 hours with a scoping call booking. No commitment required at that stage — the first call is free.
