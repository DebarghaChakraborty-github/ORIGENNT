Our Story

Born to bridge
talent gaps.

ORIGENNT was founded with a single conviction: that great organisations are built by great people, and that great people deserve a partner who takes their careers as seriously as their employers take their business.

Headquartered in the rapidly growing Infocity corridor of Bhubaneswar, we built our practice at the intersection of India's HR evolution and Odisha's emergence as a business destination. What began as a local consulting practice has expanded into a 173-service platform serving enterprises across IT/ITES, BFSI, manufacturing, e-commerce, and emerging sectors.

We are registered under the Companies Act, 2013 with core objects focused exclusively on Management Consultancy and Human Resource Functions. Our MSME verification reflects our commitment to building a lean, high-impact practice — not a bloated firm.

Our Values

The principles we
operate by.

At ORIGENNT, values aren't a wall poster. They are built into how we engage, how we deliver, and how we hold ourselves accountable — every single day.

⬡
Precision Over Volume
We believe in doing fewer things better. Every candidate we place, every strategy we deliver, reflects meticulous craft — not mass output.
⬡
Client Partnership
We embed alongside leadership teams rather than handing over reports. Implementation accountability is non-negotiable for us.
⬡
Integrity Without Exception
99.8% compliance accuracy is not a marketing number — it is our standard. We will not take shortcuts in our legal, financial, or ethical obligations.
⬡
Speed That Respects Quality
Our 12-day average time-to-hire is achieved through a pre-vetted pool of 50,000+ candidates and AI-assisted matching — not by lowering the bar.
⬡
Individual-First
Whether it is an enterprise or a fresh graduate, we treat every engagement with the same seriousness. Careers matter as much as clients.
⬡
Odisha-Proud, India-Ready
We are rooted in Bhubaneswar and proud of it. Our geographic origin does not limit our ambition — we operate pan-India and are expanding fast.
