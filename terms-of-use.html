Section 12

Dispute Resolution & Escalation Procedure

ORIGENNT prefers to resolve disputes amicably. The following escalation procedure applies to all disputes arising from or in connection with these Terms or any service engagement:

  1. Step 1 — Direct Resolution (30 days): The aggrieved party shall notify the other party in writing, setting out the nature of the dispute and the remedy sought. Both parties shall make good faith efforts to resolve the dispute within 30 days of the notice.
  2. Step 2 — Senior Management Escalation (15 days): If the dispute is not resolved at Step 1, either party may escalate the matter to a Director-level representative of ORIGENNT within 15 days of the expiry of the Step 1 period. Senior management shall meet within 15 days of escalation.
  3. Step 3 — Mediation (30 days): If the dispute remains unresolved after Step 2, either party may refer the dispute to mediation under the Mediation Act, 2023, with a mediator appointed by the Mediation Centre, High Court of Orissa. Costs of mediation shall be shared equally.
  4. Step 4 — Arbitration: If mediation fails or is declined, the dispute shall be finally resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 (as amended). The arbitration shall be conducted by a sole arbitrator. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be Bhubaneswar, Odisha. The arbitration shall be conducted in English. The award shall be final and binding.
Urgent Relief
Nothing in this dispute resolution procedure shall prevent either party from seeking urgent interlocutory relief from the courts of competent jurisdiction, including injunctions to restrain breach of confidentiality, intellectual property infringement, or other irreparable harm.
Consumer Disputes
Where a dispute involves a consumer (as defined under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019), such consumer retains the right to approach the appropriate Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.
