What is Launchpad?

Launchpad is a career services marketplace operated as a unit of ORIGENNT PRIVATE LIMITED in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. It offers 200 career services for students, freshers, job seekers and working professionals, from resume writing to interview preparation.

How much do Launchpad career services cost?

Services are priced individually, from ₹499 to ₹4,999 per service. The price, scope and session length are shown on every service before you pay.

How long is a Launchpad session?

Most services are a single session of 60–90 minutes or 90–120 minutes, depending on the service.

Do I need an account to buy a service?

No. You can enquire or pay directly without creating an account.

How can I pay?

You can pay online with Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking) or by bank transfer. Invoices and receipts name ORIGENNT PRIVATE LIMITED as the contracting entity.

Who runs Launchpad?

ORIGENNT PRIVATE LIMITED (CIN U70200OD2025PTC051453) owns and operates Launchpad. Launchpad is a brand and business unit of the company, not a separate legal entity.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Launchpad based?

ORIGENNT's operational address is 703, DLF Cyber City, Idco Info Park, Technology Corridor, Chandaka Industrial Estate, Patia, Bhubaneswar, Odisha 751031, India. Launchpad serves customers in Bhubaneswar, Odisha and across India.

What internship programs does ORIGENNT offer through Launchpad?

Three programs: Basic (₹1,499, 4 weeks), Advanced (₹4,999, 3 months, 2 live projects) and Long-Term (₹9,999, 6 months, 5 live projects). You can book a seat with a ₹1,000 token and pay the balance before Day 1.

Are the internships remote or in person?

All programs run hybrid or fully remote, so you can join from anywhere in India. Candidates in Bhubaneswar can choose on-site participation where available.

What happens after I pay the ₹1,000 token?

Your seat is reserved. You receive a confirmation email with your start date, onboarding instructions and a payment link for the balance, due at least 3 days before Day 1.

Are the internship certificates recognised?

Certificates are issued by ORIGENNT PRIVATE LIMITED, a company registered under the Companies Act, 2013. Industry affiliation certificates come with the Advanced and Long-Term programs.

Is there a job or placement guarantee?

No. Neither Launchpad nor ORIGENNT guarantees jobs, placement or income. Programs give you training, projects, certificates and career support; outcomes depend on you.

Can working professionals join the internships?

Yes. The remote formats are flexible enough to fit alongside a job.

Which policies apply to Launchpad purchases?

Launchpad operates under ORIGENNT's Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy, Refund and Cancellation Policy and Service Delivery Policy.
