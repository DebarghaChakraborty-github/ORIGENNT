Who We Are

A consultancy that
operates at the intersection

ORIGENNT was founded on a single conviction: that sustainable business performance is inseparable from organisational capability. We are a management consultancy first — one whose deepest expertise lies in the human infrastructure that makes strategy possible.

From our headquarters in Bhubaneswar, we serve enterprises across IT/ITES, BFSI, manufacturing, e-commerce and beyond. Our multidisciplinary teams combine strategic advisory with rigorous HR practice, delivering integrated solutions that create lasting competitive advantage.
