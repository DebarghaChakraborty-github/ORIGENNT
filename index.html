FREQUENTLY_ASKED_QUESTIONS

What industries do you specialize in? +
We serve diverse sectors including IT/ITES, Manufacturing, BFSI, Healthcare (non-clinical), E-commerce, Retail, and emerging startups. Our expertise spans technical and non-technical roles across all seniority levels from entry-level to C-suite positions.
How does the internship program work? +
Our internship program runs for 8-12 weeks with structured learning modules, live client project assignments, and dedicated mentorship. Interns gain hands-on experience working on real business challenges. Top performers (typically 60-70%) receive pre-placement offers with our partner organizations.
What's your typical time-to-hire? +
Our average time-to-hire is 12 days for standard positions and 18-25 days for senior/specialized roles. We maintain a pre-vetted talent pool of 50,000+ candidates and use AI-powered matching algorithms to accelerate the process by 60% compared to industry standards.
Do you handle compliance for multi-state operations? +
Yes, we manage payroll and compliance across all 28 Indian states and 8 union territories. Our legal team stays updated with state-specific labor laws, tax regulations, and statutory requirements. We provide complete audit trails, documentation, and quarterly compliance reports with 99.8% accuracy.
What's your pricing model? +
We offer flexible pricing: 8-15% of annual CTC for placements (varies by role seniority), ₹800-1,500 per-employee-per-month for payroll services, and project-based pricing for consulting engagements. Custom packages with volume discounts are available for long-term strategic partnerships.
