The Engagement Model

How a consulting
engagement actually works.

Every ORIGENNT Management Consulting engagement follows a structured 4-phase model. We don't start advising before we understand. We don't leave before change is embedded. The process below applies to every practice and every client.

01
Discovery & Diagnostic
Before any advice, we document the current state — financials, structure, people, processes, and competitive context. This becomes the baseline against which every future outcome is measured. Duration: 2–3 weeks. Deliverable: Current-State Diagnostic Report.
Weeks 1–3
02
Strategy Design & Alignment
We co-build the future-state strategy with your leadership team — not for them. This ensures ownership and dramatically improves implementation. Deliverable: Strategy Document + Execution Roadmap with KPIs.
Weeks 4–6
03
Embedded Implementation
We work alongside your team to implement the changes — attending key meetings, reviewing decisions, unblocking execution bottlenecks, and adjusting the plan as reality dictates. This is where most consultants disappear. We don't.
Month 2 → ongoing
04
Measurement & Course-Correction
Monthly reviews against the baseline. What moved. What didn't. What the data says. Adjustments made in real time, not in a quarterly report that nobody reads. Deliverable: Monthly Execution Scorecard.
Every 30 Days

You've been through a reset — a market shift, a leadership change, a failed expansion — and you need to restructure the organisation and strategy before the next attempt.
You're ready if:
Previous expansion attempts failed without clear learning
Organisation structure is misaligned with the new reality
Leadership team needs redesigning or reprioritising

Investor conversations have flagged governance gaps
Financials, HR, and operations are not diligence-ready
You need a 12-month runway to clean the house before a raise
