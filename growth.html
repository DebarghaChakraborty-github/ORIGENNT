The Problem

Why most Indian businesses
plateau — and stay plateaued.

It is not a market problem. It is not a talent problem. It is not even a funding problem. In 91% of cases ORIGENNT has diagnosed, the plateau is caused by the same structural failure: the business is running on the founder's effort, not on a system. Growth that depends on one person cannot compound.
