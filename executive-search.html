The Search Process

Eight weeks. Eight steps.
One exceptional leader.

ORIGENNT's search methodology is ISO-aligned and built on 340+ completed mandates across India. Every step is documented, reported, and delivered on schedule.

1
Week 1
Mandate Scoping & Role Brief
Deep-dive with the hiring manager, board, and relevant stakeholders to define the ideal candidate profile — competencies, culture fit, experience arc, failure factors, and non-negotiables. We also agree the process timeline, communication cadence, and decision criteria.
📄Deliverable: Signed role brief · Search agreement · Milestones confirmed
2
Week 1–2
Market Mapping & Long List Research
Our research team maps the entire talent universe for the role — identifying every senior professional in the relevant sector, function, and geography who could credibly be considered. Typically 60–120 names. This is the invisible work most firms skip; it is what makes the difference.
🗺️Deliverable: Talent map · Long list (40–60 names) with rationale
3
Week 2–3
Confidential Candidate Outreach
Direct, discreet outreach to long-listed candidates — through our network, LinkedIn, and proprietary Eastern India leadership database of 6,200+ senior professionals. No job ads. No database blasts. Individual, personalised approach for each candidate.
📬Deliverable: Weekly progress report · Interest & decline tracking
4
Week 3–4
ORIGENNT Screening & Assessment
Interested candidates undergo structured 90-minute competency interviews with ORIGENNT's search consultants. We assess against the agreed role brief — not just career narrative. Psychometric profiling (personality, leadership style, cognitive) for shortlisted candidates.
🧠Deliverable: Candidate assessment reports · Psychometric profiles · Short list (4–6 names)
5
Week 4–5
Client Presentation & First Interviews
Short-listed candidates are presented to the client with a comprehensive written profile — career rationale, assessment findings, compensation expectations, and ORIGENNT's recommendation. Client conducts first-round interviews; ORIGENNT facilitates and debriefs both sides.
📊Deliverable: Candidate dossiers · Interview facilitation · Post-interview debrief reports
6
Week 5–6
Final Interviews & Reference Checks
Final-stage candidates undergo reference checks with former managers, peers, and direct reports — structured conversations, not cursory calls. Employment, qualification, and criminal background verification. Financial background check for CFO and MD-level mandates.
✅Deliverable: Reference check reports · Background verification certificates
7
Week 6–8
Offer, Negotiation & Acceptance
ORIGENNT manages the offer process — structuring compensation, managing counter-offer risk, handling the candidate's resignation process, and navigating the notice period. 84% of ORIGENNT offers are accepted at first presentation. Counter-offer risk is assessed and mitigated before the offer is made.
🤝Deliverable: Offer letter review · Counter-offer strategy · Acceptance confirmed
8
Day 1 + 90 Days
On-boarding Support & 90-Day Guarantee
ORIGENNT remains engaged through the leader's first 90 days — conducting check-ins with both the placed leader and the client at 30, 60, and 90 days. If the placement exits for any reason within 90 days, we re-run the search at zero additional fee.
🛡️Deliverable: 30/60/90-day check-in reports · 90-day guarantee certificate

Case Study

A CFO search that three firms
failed. ORIGENNT placed in 54 days.

Infrastructure — Odisha
How ORIGENNT placed a CFO for a ₹800 Cr infrastructure group after two national firms failed over eight months
A Bhubaneswar-headquartered infrastructure group with ₹800 Cr turnover had been searching for a CFO for eight months. Two national executive search firms had presented candidates — six in total — none of whom the MD found acceptable. The role required specific project finance experience in Odisha government infrastructure contracts, familiarity with Eastern India banking relationships, and a CA who was willing to be based in Bhubaneswar rather than Hyderabad or Bengaluru.

ORIGENNT was briefed in week one of the engagement. We spent three days with the MD before approaching a single candidate — understanding not the job description, but what had been wrong with all six previous candidates. The answer was a combination of culture mismatch and insufficient regional relationship capital.

Our search identified 34 credible candidates in Eastern India specifically. We approached 22. Within 40 days we had presented four shortlisted candidates. The preferred candidate — a CA with 18 years of experience including a decade with a state PSU — joined in month three.
