Frequently Asked

Questions everyone asks
before they register.

What's the difference between Pvt Ltd and LLP?+
Both give limited liability, but Private Limited is better for investment, ESOPs, and corporate clients. LLP is better for professional service firms (CAs, lawyers, consultants) with lower compliance, no mandatory audit threshold below ₹40L turnover, and more flexibility in profit sharing. If you want to raise VC investment, Pvt Ltd is mandatory.
Can I start a Pvt Ltd alone without a co-founder?+
Yes — One Person Company (OPC) is specifically designed for this. It gives you the benefits of a Private Limited company (separate legal entity, limited liability, corporate PAN, CIN) with just one director and one shareholder. You need to nominate someone (can be a family member) who would take over if you're incapacitated.
What are government fees and are they included?+
ORIGENNT's professional fee covers our service only. Government fees — which include MCA filing fees, DSC procurement, stamp duty on MoA/AoA, and any notarisation charges — are charged separately at exact cost with receipts. These are passed through transparently, not marked up. For a Pvt Ltd, government fees typically range from ₹5,000–₹15,000 depending on authorised capital and state.
How long does registration actually take?+
Proprietorship: 2–7 days after documents. Partnership: 3–5 days. OPC / Pvt Ltd / LLP: 10–15 days after documents are verified and DSC is issued — this depends on MCA processing times, which are outside ORIGENNT's control. Section 8 and Nidhi Companies take longer due to license approvals. ORIGENNT will give you a realistic timeline upfront.
Do I need a physical office address to register?+
Yes, a registered office address is mandatory for all entity types. This can be your home address (with owner's NOC), a rented office (with rent agreement and NOC), or a coworking space (with NOC). It must be in India. You can use your home address initially and change it later as your business grows.
What happens after registration? Is ORIGENNT done?+
No — all ORIGENNT registrations include post-registration support. We provide a 12-month compliance calendar, help with GST registration, MSME/Udyam registration, bank account documentation, and a business email setup guidance. The Premium package also includes brand identity, website, and 30-day business launch consulting. We're done when you're operational, not just registered.
Is a Proprietorship "real" — or do I need a company?+
Proprietorship is a legitimate business structure recognized by Indian banks, GST authorities, and MSME registrations. Lakhs of Indian businesses operate as Proprietorships. The limitation is unlimited personal liability (your personal assets can be claimed for business debts) and inability to raise equity investment. If you're starting small, testing an idea, or running a local service business — Proprietorship is completely valid and ORIGENNT recommends it often.
Do you register trusts and NGO societies?+
We register Section 8 Companies (non-profit companies under MCA). We do not currently handle Society or Trust registrations — these are state-governed and vary significantly by state. If you need a non-profit structure with national credibility and 80G/12A eligibility, Section 8 Company is the preferred route and ORIGENNT handles it completely.
Can I change my structure later?+
Yes. Proprietorship → OPC → Pvt Ltd is a common growth path. Partnership → LLP → Pvt Ltd is also done. Conversions are legal and ORIGENNT can assist with them — though they involve fresh filings and compliance costs. The advisor dashboard above helps you choose the right structure from day one to minimise future conversion costs.
What's your refund policy if registration fails?+
If ORIGENNT causes an error in filing that leads to rejection, we refile at no extra professional fee. Government fees already paid are non-refundable by government portals, but ORIGENNT's professional fee is refunded if we cannot complete the registration due to our own error. If the MCA rejects due to name non-availability, we help with alternative names at no extra charge.
