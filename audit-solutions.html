Scope & Discovery

Live debrief session with leadership. Present the 90-day implementation roadmap and next steps.

FAQ

Everything you need to know.

Is this a statutory/financial audit?
No. ORIGENNT audits are management-level operational, HR, strategic, and business efficiency audits — not statutory financial audits under the Companies Act. We are management consultants, not Chartered Accountants. For statutory audits, engage a CA firm.
How long does an audit take?
Depending on the audit type and business size: Focused audits take 2–5 days. Business audits run 5–10 days. Enterprise and 360° audits take 10–21 days. All timelines are agreed at scope sign-off.
Is it conducted on-site or remotely?
Both options are available. Most core and specialised audits can be done remotely. Industry-specific audits (hotel, restaurant, manufacturing, retail, healthcare) require on-site visits. On-site is included for Odisha-based clients; travel actuals are charged for other locations.
What do I receive at the end?
A written findings report with an executive summary, detailed findings per domain, benchmarks against industry standards, a priority-ranked action list with rupee-level impact estimates, and a 90-day implementation roadmap. Plus a live debrief session.
Do you help with implementation after the audit?
Yes. ORIGENNT offers post-audit implementation support as a separate engagement. Many clients retain us to execute the recommendations — particularly for HR restructuring, process reengineering, and compliance remediation.
Is the audit confidential?
Completely. All audits are covered by an NDA signed before engagement commencement. Your business data, findings, and report are never shared outside the engagement team.
Can you audit a multi-location business?
Yes. Multi-location audits are available under our Bespoke Audit package. We have audited businesses with up to 9 locations simultaneously. Pricing is custom-scoped based on number of locations and required domains.
What payment methods are accepted?
All major payment methods via Razorpay — UPI, debit/credit cards, net banking, and EMI (for applicable amounts). A GST invoice is provided within 24 hours of payment. International payments available on request.
