Case Study

A sales team that forgot how to sell.

Retail FMCG — Odisha & West Bengal
How a 180-person FMCG distributor increased sales conversion by 34% through a structured L&D programme
A Bhubaneswar-headquartered FMCG distributor with 180 frontline sales representatives across Odisha and West Bengal was seeing declining conversion rates — from 38% two years ago to 24% — despite the same product quality and competitive pricing. Leadership assumed it was a market problem. ORIGENNT's Training Needs Analysis revealed it was entirely a capability problem.

Exit interview data showed that 60% of their recent sales hires had received no structured training — they were learning by watching. ORIGENNT designed and delivered a 3-module, 4-week blended sales excellence programme in Odia and English, covering consultative selling, objection handling, and CRM discipline. Field coaches reinforced learning through weekly joint calls for 8 weeks post-training.

01
Training Needs Analysis (3 weeks)
Observed 24 sales calls across Odisha and WB. Identified specific skill failures — poor needs discovery, weak objection responses, and zero CRM discipline as root causes of conversion decline.
02
Bespoke Programme Design (2 weeks)
Designed a 3-module sales programme using actual ORIGENNT-observed sales scenarios from their territory. Odia-language delivery for 140 reps; English for 40 supervisors. Role-plays used real product categories.
03
Blended Delivery (4 weeks)
Two classroom days per module interspersed with field application tasks. Post-module supervisor observations used structured checklists. Completion rate: 97%.
04
Field Coaching & 90-Day Measurement
ORIGENNT field coaches attended 8 joint sales calls per rep over 8 weeks. Conversion tracked weekly. 34% improvement confirmed by 90-day data, not self-reported scores.
