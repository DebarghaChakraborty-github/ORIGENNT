Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

Is this remote or in-person?
All programs are available in hybrid and fully remote formats. You can participate from anywhere in India. Candidates in Bhubaneswar may opt for on-site participation where available.
What happens after I pay the ₹1,000 token?
Your seat is reserved immediately. You will receive a confirmation email with your program start date, onboarding instructions, and a payment link for the balance — due at least 3 days before Day 1.
Are the certificates recognised by employers?
Yes. ORIGENNT certificates are issued by ORIGENNT Private Limited (MSME Verified, registered under Companies Act 2013). They are verified and recognised by our 200+ partner employer network. Industry Affiliation Certificates add an additional layer of professional credibility.
Can I choose my domain after enrolling?
Yes. You can specify your domain preference during enrollment or within the first 3 days of the program. Our team will guide you if you're unsure — just mention it in your onboarding form.
Who are the mentors?
All ORIGENNT mentors are practicing professionals — consultants, HR leaders, or domain experts with active industry experience. You are not coached by junior trainers or recent graduates.
What are "Live Projects"?
Live projects are actual assignments — either ORIGENNT's internal operational projects or work for our enterprise clients. They are verifiable, demonstrable, and form a real portfolio — not fabricated case studies or mock exercises.
Is there a placement guarantee?
We do not offer a placement guarantee, but our Long-Term Program's placement outcomes speak for themselves — 87% employment rate within 6 months. We provide active referrals to our enterprise client network and 12 months of career support post-program.
Can working professionals join?
Absolutely. The programs are flexible enough to accommodate working professionals — especially the remote formats. Many Advanced and Long-Term alumni have transitioned careers mid-employment.
