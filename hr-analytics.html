What Is HR Analytics

Turning headcount
into a strategic asset.

HR Analytics is the practice of collecting, structuring, and interpreting workforce data to make better business decisions. Instead of managing people on gut feel, you manage on evidence — who's likely to leave, where your hiring funnel breaks, whether your compensation is competitive, and which managers drive the most productivity.

ORIGENNT delivers this as a fully managed service — we collect the data, build the models, produce the reports, and explain what to do next. No data science team required on your side.

Case Study

A real engagement.
Real numbers. Real outcome.

BFSI — Odisha
How an NBFC with 280 employees cut attrition from 24% to 9% in 8 months
A regional NBFC headquartered in Bhubaneswar was losing relationship managers at 24% annually — their highest-performing, most expensive-to-replace employee segment. Exit interviews cited "better opportunities" generically. Leadership had no data to act on.

ORIGENNT deployed a 90-day diagnostic: mapped attrition by branch, manager, tenure band, and salary percentile. The data revealed three specific managers with 3x the team-level attrition of others, a compensation gap in the ₹4–7L band specifically in Cuttack and Berhampur branches, and a hiring funnel that was losing 60% of offer-stage candidates to a competitor paying 12% more in allowances — not base.
