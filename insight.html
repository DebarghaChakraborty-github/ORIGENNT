P1
People — Workforce Layer
Hiring is not recruitment. It is a system. When the system is absent, every hire is a gamble and every departure is a surprise.
  • Hiring consistency and criteria design
  • Onboarding structure and time-to-productivity
  • Team accountability infrastructure
  • Attrition as a system failure indicator
  • Manpower planning vs. reactive filling
P2
Process — Operational Layer
Operations without structure don't scale — they drain. Every undocumented process is operational debt compounding at growth rate.
  • Process documentation and SOP culture
  • Delegation infrastructure and failure points
  • Operational debt identification
  • Quality systems and consistency mechanisms
  • Workflow efficiency and handover design
P3
Performance — Growth Layer
Growth that feels random is random. Revenue peaks and valleys in SMEs are system failures, not market-driven outcomes.
  • Revenue system architecture
  • The revenue band ceiling problem
  • Measuring execution vs. measuring output
  • Scaling mechanisms vs. growth tactics
  • Performance management system design

Submission Guidelines

What makes a good community insight?

🎯
Name the failure specifically
Don't say "companies hire badly." Say "when hiring criteria change with each role, you cannot build a team — you can only collect individuals." Specificity is the standard.
🔍
Identify the system responsible
Every insight must trace the failure to a specific execution layer: workforce, operational, or growth. If you can't name the system, the diagnosis isn't complete.
🏗️
Show the structural fix
Don't stop at the problem. What does the structural intervention look like? What changes in the system — not just the behaviour?
🚫
No motivational content
"Work harder," "stay consistent," "believe in your team" — these don't belong here. ORIGENNT Insights is for structural analysis, not inspiration.
📊
Grounded in observed reality
Your insight should be rooted in something you've seen — a business you've run, managed, worked in, or consulted for. Abstracted theory without observed evidence doesn't meet our standard.
