Founders’ Circle in Bhubaneswar for business owners and leaders

ORIGENNT Founders’ Circle brings founders, entrepreneurs, SME owners and senior professionals together for curated business roundtables in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Ask ORIGENNT ↗
Is the annual membership fee the same as the meetup fee?
No. Annual membership is ₹49,999 per year. An individual meetup is ₹1,499. These are separate payments and one does not automatically include the other.
Does payment guarantee membership or a seat?
No. Membership applications are subject to review. Each roundtable has a maximum of 10 selected participants, and membership does not guarantee attendance at every session. Confirm the applicable session before paying an individual meetup fee.
When and where are the roundtables held?
The planned format is Friday, 7:30–10:00 AM IST, at ORIGENNT Conference Hall, Bhubaneswar. Confirm the specific session date and venue arrangements with ORIGENNT before making a meetup payment.
What happens after a successful payment?
After the payment is verified by the server and successfully recorded by the configured payment-record system, a unique reference code should be shown. Keep that code for follow-up. A browser redirect or payment screenshot alone is not proof of verification.
What if money is debited but no reference code appears?
Do not immediately pay again. Keep your Razorpay payment ID or transaction reference and contact ORIGENNT so the transaction can be checked and reconciled.
How can an SME contact ORIGENNT for other services?
Use the main ORIGENNT contact page for consulting, workforce, operations, implementation and other business requirements. Founders’ Circle membership and service engagements are separate.

Payment and participation notice. Confirm current programme terms, session availability, cancellation/refund conditions and any applicable taxes with ORIGENNT before payment. Do not assume a refund policy or guaranteed attendance where no written term has been provided.
